# Words that may appear INSIDE a WordNet definition without disqualifying the word being
# defined — but that still may never be an answer or an option themselves.
#
# The definition scan does two jobs and they are not the same job. It keeps an unsafe SENSE
# out of the child's sight, which is right: `crack` and `pot` are innocent lemmas carrying
# senses a nine-year-old's quiz has no business in. It also decides whether a WORD exists at
# all, and there it does collateral damage, because WordNet's phrasing is a lexicographer's.
#
# `valley` is "a long depression in the surface of the land". `clan` and `akin` are "related
# by blood". `cattle` is "domesticated bovine animals... regardless of sex". Each of those
# words is entirely safe and each was absent from the lexicon. Measured: 142 common words
# whose lemma is clean and whose every sense trips this scan.
#
# So a word on this list stops a definition from being SHOWN — `valley` gets no definition
# question — without stopping the word from existing. Everything not on this list still does
# both, which is why the sexual vocabulary, the slurs and the drugs are not here: a word
# whose only meaning is one of those should not exist in a children's lexicon at all.
